Our Story

Building beyond what seems possible.

A faith-driven founder accelerator ecosystem for the world’s next unicorn founders.

Foundational Scripture

“What no eye has seen, what no ear has heard, and what no human mind has conceived, the things God has prepared for those who love him.”
1 Corinthians 2:9 (NIV)

01

Who We Are

A faith-driven founder accelerator ecosystem for the world’s next unicorn founders.

We curate transformational “Upper Room” spaces and experiences that bring together exceptional, hand-selected founders and builders to encounter the Holy Spirit and leave equipped and empowered to do mighty exploits.

We give life and legs to God-sized visions and ventures: championing Kingdom principles, cultivating Kingdom relationships, and unlocking the resources needed to turn God-given ideas into God-sized impact.

02

Who We’re For

For those called to build category-defining visions and ventures with impact and scale.

We seek out and hand-select exceptional founders and builders carrying category-defining visions and world-changing, future-shaping missions. Inspired by 1 Corinthians 2:9, we believe God is calling a generation of visionary founders to pioneer what no eye has seen, no ear has heard, and no mind has conceived.

We are building for those called to create what doesn’t exist yet: to pioneer new categories, transform industries, solve consequential problems, and build ventures that shape the future.

03

Our Conviction

Building beyond what seems possible.

We believe the next generation of world-changing ventures will emerge from Kingdom-minded builders who dare to dream beyond what seems possible: founders who build in partnership with the Holy Spirit, pursue excellence without compromise, and faithfully steward their God-given visions into ventures that create lasting Kingdom impact.
