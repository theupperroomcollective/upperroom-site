Run With Purpose. Build for Eternity.

For Christian founders, funders, builders and visionaries.

We’re Not Just Running. We’re Running to Win.

Register for a Run See the six Saturdays

Are you building for more than personal success, influence, or legacy?

Are you called to create ventures that transform lives, advance God’s Kingdom, and make an impact that echoes into eternity?

Then this is your kind of run.

The Upper Room Collective Founder Run Club brings together faith-driven founders, funders, innovators, and builders across the San Francisco Bay Area to move with purpose, build Kingdom relationships, and champion one another in the race we’ve been called to run.

Because we’re not just building businesses. We’re building for eternity.

What the Run Club offers

  • Run With Purpose

    A Saturday morning run grounded in faith, movement, and intentional living.

  • Find Your People

    Build authentic relationships with Christian founders, funders, and builders who share your convictions and calling.

  • Build Beyond Yourself

    Encourage one another to create ventures that serve people, solve problems, and scale Kingdom impact.

  • Run to Win

    Pursue excellence, discipline, and eternal purpose together, one stride at a time.
