The Founders' Table.

Exceptional People. Meaningful Conversations. Kingdom Relationships.

Where exceptional founders break bread. An intimate gathering of visionary founders and funders. Exceptional food, meaningful conversation, and Kingdom relationships built around a thoughtfully curated table.

Introduce Yourself

Some of the Best Things Are Built Around a Table.

Great ideas rarely grow in isolation. They take shape through honest conversations, unexpected connections, and the people who believe enough to build alongside us.

At The Upper Room Collective, we believe in the power of breaking bread.

We bring together a small, thoughtfully curated circle of faith-driven founders, funders, and builders to share a meal, exchange ideas, challenge perspectives, and explore what God might make possible when extraordinary people gather.

No stages. No pitches. No performance.

Just good food, remarkable people, candid conversations, and room for something extraordinary to begin.

Because sometimes the most significant thing you build is a relationship.
