03 · Empower · Founder Design Studio

From Revelation to Reality.

Every category-defining venture begins with a vision. Bringing it to life requires clarity, intentional design, and the strategy to build.

The Founder Design Studio partners with visionary founders to clarify their calling, shape their brand, and develop the strategic foundations needed to transform God-sized ideas into world-changing ventures.

Request a Consultation

Our Philosophy

Build What Doesn’t Exist Yet.

We don’t just design brands. We help founders design the foundations for what they’re called to build.

Extraordinary ventures begin with conviction, take shape through intentional design, and come to life through courageous execution.

At the Founder Design Studio, we go beyond aesthetics to help founders articulate what they’re building, why it matters, whom it serves, and what makes it different.

Rooted in Kingdom principles and informed by strategic business thinking, our approach connects purpose with positioning, creativity with commercial strategy, and revelation with execution.

What We Do

Strategy, identity and foundations, designed together.

  • 01

    Vision and Brand Strategy

    Define the vision, mission, purpose, target audience, customer problem, value proposition, and positioning that make a venture distinct.

  • 02

    Brand Identity and Creative Direction

    Translate strategy into a compelling verbal and visual identity through brand personality, messaging, color, typography, and creative guidance.

  • 03

    Business and Venture Design

    Shape the offering, customer experience, business priorities, and go-to-market direction needed to move from concept toward market readiness.

  • 04

    Founder Advisory and Execution

    Move from strategy into action through collaborative consulting, structured milestones, accountability, and implementation planning.

Our Signature Consulting Experience

Called to Create.

A Kingdom Brand Development Consulting Program

A guided consulting experience for founders ready to move beyond ideas and intentionally build the identity, strategy, and foundations of their venture.

The 52-Day Build.

From Revelation to Realization.

Inspired by Nehemiah 6:15, our 52-Day Build provides a focused framework for turning a founder’s vision into a clear brand strategy, creative direction, and actionable roadmap.

Discover → Define → Design → Develop → Deploy

  1. Stage 1

    Discover

    Vision, mission, purpose, audience, and the problem you exist to solve.

  2. Stage 2

    Define

    Positioning, unique value proposition, and brand strategy.

  3. Stage 3

    Design

    Visual identity, personality, voice, and customer experience.

  4. Stage 4

    Develop

    Brand assets, business priorities, and an implementation plan.

  5. Stage 5

    Deploy

    Execution milestones and a founder check-in to carry the vision forward.

Called to Create is the consulting experience; the 52-Day Build is its structured framework. Full website development, creative asset production, and ongoing consulting are scoped separately.

Who We Build With

For Those Called to Create What Doesn’t Exist Yet.

We partner with ambitious, purpose-driven founders who see beyond what exists today and are committed to building what could exist tomorrow.

Whether you’re shaping an early-stage idea, launching a new venture, or repositioning an existing business, the Founder Design Studio is designed for builders seeking strategic clarity, differentiated positioning, and intentional growth.

We look for founders with bold vision, a commitment to excellence, a heart to serve people, and the courage to build differently.

The Founder Experience

Intentional by Design. Personal by Nature.

Our consulting approach is collaborative, tailored, and rooted in relationship. We don’t believe in one-size-fits-all strategies. Every engagement begins with understanding the founder, the vision, and the opportunity.

From there, we combine strategic discovery, thoughtful design, and actionable planning to build a foundation that is both meaningful and commercially relevant.

  • Guided discovery
  • Collaborative strategy sessions
  • Customized brand development tools
  • An actionable execution roadmap
