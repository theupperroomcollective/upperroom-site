The Upper Room Collective ("we", "us", "our") is a faith-driven founder accelerator ecosystem based in the San Francisco Bay Area. We respect your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through this website, how we use it, who helps us handle it, and the choices you have.

## Information we collect

**Information you give us.** When you complete a form on this site, we collect the details you choose to share. Depending on the form, this can include:

- your name, business name, email address and phone number
- your website and LinkedIn profile
- what you are building, and anything else you write in a message
- which experiences or updates you are interested in, such as consulting, masterclasses or Run Club
- your communications consent: whether you agreed to hear from us, the wording you agreed to, and when

**Information collected automatically.** We use Fathom Analytics to understand how many people visit and which pages are useful. Fathom does not use cookies, does not collect personal information, and does not track you across other websites. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type and the time of a request) for security and to keep the site running.

We do not use advertising cookies or tracking pixels.

## How we use your information

- To reply to your enquiry or consultation request
- To send the updates you asked for, such as news about masterclasses, Run Club and other experiences
- To invite you to experiences that match the interests you shared
- To keep a record of your consent and your communication preferences
- To understand, in aggregate, how the website is used so we can improve it
- To protect the site and our community from spam and misuse

## Email and other communications

We only send updates and invitations if you have agreed to receive them. Every email we send includes a way to unsubscribe, and you can also ask us to stop at any time by emailing [tanya@the5collective.org](mailto:tanya@the5collective.org). If you unsubscribe, we may still contact you about a request you made, such as a consultation you asked for.

## How we share information

We do not sell or rent your personal information, and we do not share it with other organizations for their own marketing.

We use a small number of trusted service providers to run this website and our communications. They handle your information only on our behalf:

- **Google** (Google Sheets and Google Apps Script) stores form submissions in a private spreadsheet that only our team can access.
- **Vercel** hosts this website.
- **Fathom Analytics** provides privacy-friendly, cookie-free visitor statistics.
- **An email service provider** sends our newsletters and updates.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect the rights, safety or property of our community or others.

## Links to other websites

This site links to other services, such as event registration pages and social media. Their own privacy policies apply when you use them.

## How long we keep information

We keep your information for as long as we need it for the purposes above, or until you ask us to delete it. We keep a record of your consent and unsubscribe requests for as long as needed to respect your choices.

## How we protect information

Access to submissions is limited to the people on our team who need it, and we use reputable providers with strong security practices. No method of storing or sending information online is completely secure, but we work to protect what you share with us.

## Your choices and rights

You can ask us to:

- tell you what information we hold about you
- correct or update your information
- delete your information
- stop sending you updates (withdraw your consent)

Email [tanya@the5collective.org](mailto:tanya@the5collective.org) and we will respond within a reasonable time. Depending on where you live (for example, California or the European Union), you may have additional rights under local law, and we will honor them.

## Children

This website is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has shared information with us, please contact us and we will delete it.

## Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. If the changes are significant, we will let people on our mailing list know.

## Contact us

Questions about this policy or your information: [tanya@the5collective.org](mailto:tanya@the5collective.org).
